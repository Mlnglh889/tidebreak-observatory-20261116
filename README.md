An original, offline 3-vs-3 tactical FPS in an Atlantic tidal observatory. Sanded concrete, copper machinery and luminous tidal instruments frame two flanking lanes and a central observation court. Every shape and sound is generated by the game's own source; no commercial assets are used.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome with WebGL2. Click Enter the observatory, buy equipment, then press Enter to deploy. The first click captures the mouse. Escape releases it and pauses; Resume continues.

WASD moves, mouse aims, left click fires, R reloads, C crouches, Shift walks quietly, G throws a bouncing frag. Hold E near an instrument to plant (2.5 seconds) or near the charge to disarm (4 seconds). During buy phase, 1–5 buy equipment; B closes or opens the shop. Enter advances a result screen or restarts a completed operation.

Breakers win by eliminating Wardens or detonating a charge at A or B. Wardens win by eliminating unplanted Breakers, disarming, or running out the round clock. A planted charge keeps the round alive even after every Breaker falls. First to four wins; the player changes sides after three rounds. Credits carry across rounds: elimination 300, victory 2400, loss 1600. Carbine, sidearm and marksman each have distinct magazines, reserves, recoil, spread, damage and reload times. Head hits deal 3.5 times body damage. Moving increases dispersion; crouching reduces height and speed.

The two squadmates and three opponents use shared collision and line-of-sight geometry, a route graph, sound investigation, attack/retreat ranges and autonomous plant/disarm behavior. No external requests are needed after the local files load. Audio uses Web Audio oscillators unlocked by the start click.

## Source and delivery

- game/map.js: architectural solids, objective locations, navigation graph and visibility.
- game/sim.js: state, seeded randomness, fixed-step combat, economy and rounds.
- game/view.js: three.js scene, original geometric characters, instruments, viewmodel and pooled effects.
- game/main.js: input, 60 Hz accumulator, HUD, radar, synthesized audio and read-only playtest observations.
- main.ts: copies static files and screenshots, builds the exact kit dossier using unchanged vendor/makefun-threejs.ts. Node 22.18+ standard library only.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/tidebreak-output node main.ts
```

Three.js 0.187.0 and its MIT license are included unchanged from the competition kit. Screenshots are unedited browser JPEG captures at 1280×720. The rendering resolution is scaled for software GPUs; interface remains native resolution.
